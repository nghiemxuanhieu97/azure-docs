# Question 1
Capital Expenditure describes an action of spending company funds on renting services/infrastructure
over time.
- [ ] True
- [x] False
>**Note:** Capital Expenditure describes an action of spending company funds **upfront**

# Question 2
In an Operational Expenditure model you can deduct tax ______.
- [ ] Over time
- [x] In the same year
- [ ] In the same month
- [ ] At the moment of the purchase
>**Note:** In Operational Expenditure model you spend money on services every month as you use
them. As such you deduct the tax **in the same year**. This might vary in certain countries though.

# Question 3
Having you own datacenter infrastructure has a big ___ cost associated.
- [x] Initial
- [ ] Over time
- [ ] Initial and over time
>**Note:** Your own infrastructure means you need to purchase servers. Invest in the server room, cooling,
 networking, etc. This of course costs money, which means you need to invest a lot of money **initial**.
Later your costs are relatively low.
